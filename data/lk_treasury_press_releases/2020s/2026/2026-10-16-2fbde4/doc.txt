Pre-Bid Meeting: Domestic Dollar Bond (DDB) 2026

The Public Debt Management Office (PDMO) of the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development is preparing to issue the Domestic Dollar Bond (DDB) 2026, a United States Dollar-denominated debt instrument offered in the domestic market. The issue forms a part of the Government’s efforts to diversify its funding sources and to deepen the local market for government securities. The DDB 2026 will be issued through 15 Designated Agents (DAs), comprising Primary Dealers and Licensed Commercial Banks.

In preparation for the issue, the PDMO convened a pre-bid meeting on 16 October 2026, from 9.30 a.m. to 10.30 a.m. All 15 Designated Agents and the Paying Agent participated for this meeting.

The meeting provided a forum for participants to seek clarification on the operational and regulatory aspects of this issuance. Officers from the Foreign Exchange Department of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, and Fiscal Policy Department and Treasury Operations Department of the General Treasury, were also present at this meeting.
