PRESS RELEASE

ADB Technical Assistance Workshop on Liability Management, Investor Engagement and Sovereign Credit Ratings

Director General of PDMO with the representatives of ADB, technical resource persons, and officers from PDMO and CBSL

The Public Debt Management Office (PDMO) of the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development is engaged in a three-day workshop on Liability Management, Investor Engagement and Sovereign Credit Ratings from 18 to 20 November 2026 at Hilton Colombo under the Technical Assistance of Asian Development Bank (ADB).

The workshop brings together officers from PDMO, the Central Bank of Sri Lanka and other relevant government institutions, with the participation of ADB Senior Treasury Specialists Mr. Daniel Heuberger and Ms. Dipika Darshani. ADB has provided expert assistance for supporting PDMO’s capacity-building initiatives under ADB Technical Assistance.

The workshop forms part of ADB’s continued technical assistance to strengthen PDMO’s institutional and operational capacity, support effective public debt management, enhance investor confidence and contribute to Sri Lanka’s efforts to improve sovereign creditworthiness.
